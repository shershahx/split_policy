1. Scope and operator

Split It is operated by Sher Shah in Islamabad, Pakistan. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us. It distinguishes current functionality from features still in development.

Privacy contact: splitit.privacy@gmail.com.
Support: splitit.support@gmail.com.

2. Information currently processed

  • Account information: email address, display name, and password supplied during registration or sign-in. Supabase Auth handles password verification and storage; we do not intentionally log or store plaintext passwords in the app.
  • Authentication information: user ID, session/access/refresh tokens, and temporary PKCE verification material used for confirmation and recovery links.
  • Local preferences: theme selection saved on your device.
  • Support communications: information you choose to send by email.
  • Operational request information: Supabase and email-delivery providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, authentication/security events, and related technical logs to deliver and protect the service.

No advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDK is currently configured. The app does not currently access your contacts or location, process payments, or collect bank-account or payment-card details.

3. Shared-expense features in development

The app contains screens and database definitions for groups, memberships, invitations, friends, expenses, splits, and settlements. Their Flutter repositories are not yet connected to the cloud database and do not currently save those records. Do not rely on this version as your only expense record.

When shared-data features are enabled, records you submit may include names, invited email addresses, group membership, expense descriptions, monetary amounts, dates, payers, recipients, and split allocations. Relevant group members will be able to view shared records. Receipt uploads, OCR, and notifications are not currently implemented; their data handling will be described before activation.

4. Why we process information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, deliver confirmation and recovery emails, maintain sessions, save preferences, answer support requests, and detect or prevent misuse. Shared-data features, when enabled, will use information to display shared expenses and balances.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or disclose it to advertising companies. Where applicable, processing may rely on providing the requested service, legitimate security/support interests, legal obligations, or consent for an optional feature. Applicable law determines the legal basis.

5. Providers and disclosures

We use Supabase Auth and a Supabase PostgreSQL project. Account credentials and profile information are processed by Supabase. Read Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Confirmation and recovery emails are delivered through the project's configured email provider. Our support/privacy mailboxes are hosted by Gmail. Your app store and email provider have their own policies.

We may disclose information to service providers needed to operate the app, as required by law, to protect users or enforce lawful terms, or at your request. Shared records may be disclosed to their intended group participants when those features are enabled.

6. Storage and security

Cloud information is processed in the selected Supabase project region and may be processed in other countries by service providers. The actual project region is set by the operator, not automatically determined by the app.

The Android implementation uses HTTPS connections, PKCE callback flows, and platform-protected local storage for sessions and PKCE verifiers. Theme preferences use SharedPreferences. Hive is initialized, but an encrypted expense cache or offline synchronization system is not implemented.

The supplied database schema enables Row Level Security. Its policies and future data operations still require security testing before production use. No system is completely secure; these measures do not guarantee that misuse or unauthorized access can never occur.

7. Retention and deletion

Account/profile information is generally kept while your account remains in use. Local sessions can remain on your device until sign-out, expiry, or removal of app data. Provider logs and backups follow configured/provider retention practices.

There is not yet an in-app account-deletion or data-export workflow. Send requests to our privacy email. We may verify your identity and explain any legal, backup, or shared-record retention limitations. No fixed deletion deadline is promised by this policy; applicable legal deadlines still apply.

Future shared records may need to be retained or de-identified to preserve other participants' history. Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account.

8. Your choices and rights

You can change your display name, reset your password, sign out, and change your theme. Contact us to request access, correction, export, restriction, objection, or deletion, where those rights apply. You may complain to your local data protection authority and will not be penalized for exercising applicable rights.

For California residents, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Rights depend on whether the relevant law applies to the operator and your circumstances. This policy does not claim certification or blanket GDPR/CCPA compliance.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 or below the applicable minimum age in their country. Registration includes a self-declared age checkbox; this is not independent age verification. A parent or guardian should contact us if a child has provided information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Local storage, tracking, and transfers

The native Android app does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. It does store authentication material and preferences locally. A browser version may use browser storage for authentication; browser storage is not a guarantee of protection against malicious scripts.

International processing may occur depending on your location and the providers. Applicable safeguards and legal requirements must be assessed for the deployment; using the app alone is not treated as a blanket waiver of data-protection rights.

11. Updates and contact

We may revise this policy when functionality or providers change. The revision label identifies this version. Material changes will be communicated by a reasonable available channel; an in-app notification system is not yet implemented.
