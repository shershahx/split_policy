Split It — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 30, 2026

Welcome to Split It! We built this app to make splitting expenses simple and stress-free. Because your financial data is personal, we designed Split It with a privacy-first approach.

This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe. In short: we don't sell your data, and we use secure Huawei Mobile Services (HMS) to keep everything running smoothly.

By downloading or using Split It through the Huawei AppGallery, you agree to this Privacy Policy and confirm you are at least 13 years old.

📂 1. What Information Do We Collect?

We only collect the absolute minimum data required to make the app work.

Information You Give Us

To create an account and use the app, you provide:

  • Account Info: Your email address and display name.
  • Groups & Expenses: Group names, expense descriptions, payment amounts, and who paid whom.
  • Friends: Names and emails of friends you add to the app to track balances.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use the app, some basic technical data is handled in the background to keep things secure:

  • Device Info: Basic device details (like OS version) to ensure the app runs properly.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure, automated keys generated by Huawei AGConnect to keep you logged in safely.
🚫 What We Do NOT Collect
  • No Location Tracking: We never track where you are.
  • No Contacts Access: We never upload or scrape your phone's address book.
  • No Browsing Data: We do not track what you do outside of Split It.

🎯 2. How Do We Use Your Information?

Your data is used entirely to power the app's features. We use it to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Calculate who owes what and track your group expenses.
  • Sync your balances across your devices.
  • Fix crashes and improve the app's performance.
❌ NEVER sell your personal info
💳 No credit card processing (cash/external only)

🔒 3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We rely on state-of-the-art security provided by Huawei Mobile Services (HMS) to protect your information.

  • Where it's stored: Your data is securely stored in Huawei AGConnect Cloud DB.
  • Encryption: All data sent between your phone and the cloud is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.
  • Local Storage: App settings (like dark mode) and cached data are securely stored on your device using an encrypted local database.
  • Retention: We retain your account data (groups, expenses, friends) for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.
  • Future Features (On-Device Processing): If we introduce Receipt Scanning in the future, the Optical Character Recognition (OCR) will run entirely on your device. Photos of your receipts will never be sent to our servers.

🤝 4. Third-Party Services (Huawei SDKs)

Split It is built exclusively for the Huawei ecosystem and relies on the following Huawei services to function. These SDKs collect necessary data to ensure the app works.

Service (SDK) Function / Data Collected
Huawei Account Kit Enables "Sign in with Huawei ID." Collects OpenID and basic profile info (avatar, nickname) only when you log in.
AGConnect Auth Manages your secure login (Email or Huawei ID). Collects authentication tokens and user IDs.
AGConnect Cloud DB Syncs your groups, expenses, and friend balances across devices. Stores the data you input (groups, expenses).
AGConnect Crash & APM Collects anonymized crash logs and performance data (e.g., app version, device model, OS version) to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

For more details on how Huawei handles data, please review the Huawei Consumer Privacy Policy.

🗑️ 5. Your Rights and Managing Your Data

You maintain full control over your personal data.

  • Viewing & Editing: You can update your display name directly from the Profile screen inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting Your Data: You can delete your account directly within the app via Settings > Delete Account. Once requested, your account is permanently removed from our active database within 30 days. (You may also request deletion by emailing us).
  • Requesting a Copy: You have the right to request a digital copy of all personal data we hold about you.
🌍 EU/UK and California Residents: Split It fully respects your rights under GDPR and CCPA. You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and we guarantee you will never be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

👶 6. Children's Privacy

Split It is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can delete the account.

📝 7. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or changes in legal requirements.

  • Minor changes will just update the "Last Updated" date at the top.
  • If we make significant changes to how we handle your data, we will notify you through the app before the changes take effect.
